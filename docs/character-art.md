# Character illustrations — focused watercolor studies

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Final WebP assets live in `public/assets/characters/`; all twelve were visually inspected together. The original PNGs are preserved in the generator output directory. Format conversion and sizing preserve the full composition.

## Shared final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: educational watercolor illustration used in a Chinese-character flashcard. Subject: [subject below]. Style: sophisticated traditional Chinese watercolor and ink wash, hand-painted on very pale warm ivory rice paper (#f6f1e7), gentle paper texture, muted ink blue gray and sage palette with appropriate warm accent for the subject. Composition: horizontal 3:2, object centered with 10 percent clear margins, subject is large and instantly recognizable. This is an object study, not a scenic landscape or UI. Delicate imperfect pigment edges, sparse confident brushwork, softly fade surrounding paper to clean ivory. No letters, no Chinese characters, no words, no writing, no seals, no typography, no borders, no UI, no watermark.
